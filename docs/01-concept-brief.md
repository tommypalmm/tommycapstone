# 01 · Concept brief

**Milestone.** CP-M1 · Sep 9
**Status.** Draft

## Working title

The Pitch

## One sentence

A whole backend system that manages all the moving parts of my business and
daily life, for my consulting/marketing business.

## Who it's for

Me and my business partner.

## The job it does

When I have tasks due for my business, I want to complete them the most
efficient way, so I can make money and be satisfied.

## The problem

Running a consulting/marketing business means a lot of moving parts across the
business and daily life, and staying on top of all of them is hard when they're
scattered.

## Current workaround

Usually a spreadsheet or a task manager.

## Why this, why now

I already run the business, so I know the problem firsthand, and I can build it
myself with Cursor.

## In scope

- One backend system that manages the moving parts of the business and daily
  life.
- Getting tasks done in the most efficient way.

## Out of scope

- Managing every single aspect right away — start with what I can actually
  connect.

## What success looks like

Tasks get done the most efficient way, I make money, and I'm satisfied that
nothing is slipping.

## Risks

I don't know if it can manage every aspect — hopefully I can get access to all
the APIs I'd need.

## Stack guess

I would build it with Cursor.

## Open questions

- Which APIs can I actually get access to?
