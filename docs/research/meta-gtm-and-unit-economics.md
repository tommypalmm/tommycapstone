# Meta GTM and unit economics

**Date.** Sep 18, 2026

## What we are advertising

The **software**, to studio owners, nationwide. Not Palmm ads that put cars in a driveway. Separate brand, separate Meta ad account.

## Is “one product, sell as much as possible on Meta” possible?

Yes if:

- One vertical sentence (pilates / Lagree)
- The click lands **in a demo studio** (book a fake 9am in 30 seconds)
- Price ~$149–249 so CAC $400–800 payback in ~12 months
- Product actually runs (spots, waitlist, Stripe)

No if:

- Ad says “OS for any service business” (Jobber wins)
- Funnel is book-a-Zoom (no-shows → $2k–8k CAC)
- Product is Grand Prix with a new coat of paint

## CAC (corrected)

$5k per paying customer is a **failure case** (leaky demo calls), not the plan.

Rough $1,000 spend, product-led:

- ~$3 CPC → ~15% open demo → ~25% create a studio → ~15% pay → about **$500–800 CAC**

Leaky sales-led: **$2k–8k**.

Targets: **$400–800** healthy; **under $1,500** while learning; **shut off at $2,000+**.

B2B SaaS paid social medians are higher when demo-led; vertical SaaS payback medians are longer, but LTV/CAC is better if they stay. Studios stay if billing doesn’t break.

## Funnel

1. Creative: waitlist SMS + reformer map. Not a feature grid.
2. Audience: US independent studio owners; lookalikes of `studio_created`.
3. Destination: demo tenant.
4. Events: `demo_opened` → `studio_created` → `paid`. Optimize for studio created.
5. Offer: 14-day sandbox, card on file; concierge import as the upgrade to go live.

## Sequence (do not skip)

1. One booking table, tenant_id, Stripe, week grid + public booking.
2. Demo studio generator.
3. 3–5 real studios (not Grand Prix as the only one).
4. Then Meta to owners.

Until step 3, ads train the pixel on churn.
