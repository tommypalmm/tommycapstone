# 0002 · Auto spas are a Palmm client, not the SaaS TAM

**Date.** 2026-09-18
**Status.** Accepted

**Context.**

Grand Prix Auto Spa has a real scheduler. Appearance shops (PPF/tint/wrap) fit Palmm’s ICP. Most “auto spas” are mobile detailers who will not pay $200+/mo. ~50k listings vs a few thousand serious shops.

**Decision.**

Keep Grand Prix as a Palmm systems engagement. Do not point Meta ads for this capstone at detailing vans. Do not make this repo a fork of `grand-pit-scheduler`.

**Why not the alternative.**

Nationwide Meta needs owners who already live in the feed and already pay for software. Boutique studios match that; mobile detail does not.

**What would change my mind.**

A paid waitlist of 100+ brick-and-mortar PPF/tint shops asking for the Grand Prix admin as SaaS at $299/mo.
