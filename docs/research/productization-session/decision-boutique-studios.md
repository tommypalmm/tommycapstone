# 0001 · Independent boutique studios, not a generic OS

**Date.** 2026-09-18
**Status.** Accepted

**Context.**

We asked whether Grand Prix’s admin/booking backend could be productized for any service business and sold nationwide on Meta. Auto spas, med spas, music academies, dealers, epoxy, and pilates were compared.

**Decision.**

Capstone product is a **boutique studio OS**. First niche: independent pilates / Lagree / barre. One codebase. Ads name pilates.

**Why not the alternative.**

Generic service OS loses to Jobber on Meta. Med spa is Zenoti/Boulevard. Music is Jackrabbit at $15–99/mo. Auto spa payers are too few and too mobile. Dealers are a different product (inventory).

**What would change my mind.**

A design partner studio refuses the category, or interviews show independent pilates will not leave Mindbody without a native app we cannot build in v1.
