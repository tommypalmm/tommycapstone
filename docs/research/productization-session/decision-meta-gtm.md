# 0003 · One product, Meta as demo engine, no marketplace

**Date.** 2026-09-18
**Status.** Accepted

**Context.**

Plan was to “productize and sell as much as possible through Meta ads.” Sales-led B2B funnels can hit $2k–8k CAC. Mindbody taxes studios via marketplace and metered SMS.

**Decision.**

- Ad destination is a **demo studio**, not a Zoom form.
- Target CAC **$400–800**; kill campaigns above **$2,000**.
- Price band **$149–249/mo**, Stripe standard rates, **no consumer marketplace**.
- SMS/reviews/winbacks **included by default**; loyalty is a milestone text, not the headline.
- Palmm ad account is never this product’s ad account.

**Why not the alternative.**

Self-serve $49 fights QuoteIQ-class tools. Demo-only $399 wastes the click. Marketplace fees are the #1 Mindbody grievance we can honestly attack.

**What would change my mind.**

Demo-shop conversion under 2% after creative testing, or Stripe Connect + 10DLC proving too heavy for capstone — then capstone ships scheduling only and SMS is mocked.
