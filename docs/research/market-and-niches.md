# Market and niches

**Date.** Sep 17–18, 2026
**Source.** Working session on Grand Prix scheduler + whether to productize an OS and sell it on Meta.
**Not interviews.** Field notes. Do not treat as customer quotes.

## Verdict

Build **one boutique studio OS**. Advertise pilates / Lagree. Barre is the same app. Recovery (plunge/sauna rooms) is a later booking mode. Grand Prix stays a Palmm client. Do not advertise auto-spa software nationwide.

## How to score a niche

Owners on Meta, brick-and-mortar, will pay $150+, enough of them, no ServiceTitan/Zenoti, Palmm can reach them.

---

### 1. Boutique studios (pilates, Lagree, barre) — GO

Physical. Instagram *is* the business. They already buy Meta ads. Reformers don’t go in a van. Software budget **$150–400/mo**. Thousands of independents. ~11% of pilates still on paper or Acuity (PilatesBridge survey: Mindbody 33.6%, Acuity 15.6%, pen and paper 11.5%).

Mindbody is hated for fees and marketplace cuts. Momence, Walla, Mariana Tek, Arketa, WellnessLiving, StudioGrowth, PushPress compete. **Audience is right; category is not empty.** Win on honest price, included SMS, no marketplace tax, 7-day switch.

Skip Club Pilates, Solidcore, Pure Barre, StretchLab, F45, Orangetheory, CycleBar — corporate picks the tool.

**TAM vs auto spa:** not a unicorn. Enough for a $2M ARR product if you execute. Same “small but paying” shape as premium appearance shops, except owners are actually reachable.

### 2. Recovery / plunge / sauna — second studio type

Same IG owner. Booking is **rooms by the hour**, not a class. Arketa is one of the few that models this. Add after pilates works.

### 3. Yoga, cycling, boxing, stretch, HIIT (independent) — same app later

Same class engine. Don’t lead ads with “all boutique fitness.” Pixel needs one sentence: pilates.

### 4. Appearance shops (PPF, tint, wrap, ceramic, detail) — Palmm only

Grand Prix is a real design partner. ~50k US “detailing” listings; most are vans that will not pay $299. Paying shops (roof + PPF/tint/wrap) are a few thousand. Fine as a client system. Thin as a Meta SaaS TAM. Three booking tables in the current app (`bookings`, `appointments`, `detailing_bookings`) — see [`grand-prix-scheduler-lessons.md`](grand-prix-scheduler-lessons.md).

### 5. Independent used-car lots — company two

Velocity-shaped. Facebook more than Reels. **$400–800/mo** possible. Tens of thousands of independents. DealerCenter is the default and looks old. Product is inventory/leads/recon — not this scheduler. Harder build, bigger pond.

### 6. Dog daycare / boarding — backup if fitness is refused

Owners post all day. Buildings with occupancy. Gingr ~5k facilities; industry much larger. Van groomers = the auto-spa problem (many, cheap). Pay less than pilates.

### 7. Med spas — no

Most IG-native, highest software bills. Zenoti (~30k beauty/wellness), Boulevard ($80M Series D), PatientNow (EMR). HIPAA, injectables, charting. Best audience, worst category.

### 8. Music academies — no

~12k–16k US music schools. Jackrabbit is the school default. My Music Staff owns teachers at ~$15/mo. Opus1/Classcard already attack “Jackrabbit is ugly.” **$15–99/mo** kills Meta CAC that works at $199. Different product (tuition, makeups, families). Gigs is a design partner, not a market. Only interesting as academy + venue, still a different company.

### 9. Also no

- Epoxy / garage floor — QuoteIQ, CoatingOS, Jobber
- Marine detailing — tiny, QuoteIQ/Urable/Yacht Logic
- HVAC / plumbing / cleaning — ServiceTitan / Jobber
- Generic “service business OS” — Jobber ads win

## Auto spa numbers (why we walked away as TAM)

- US wash + detailing roughly $17–19B, highly fragmented
- ~50k detailing service listings vs a few thousand serious PPF/ceramic/tint shops
- Detailing software: QuoteIQ ~$30, Urable $45–166, Jobber, Tintify ~$163–195, ShopHawk $49–199, SalesThumb, TintFlow, TintWiz, OrbisX, RINZ
- No Boulevard, but also no dense set of owners who will pay and are brick-and-mortar

## Palmm vs separate company

Palmm GTM is ads that fill the **shop’s** calendar. This product’s ads fill **your** pipeline of studio owners. Separate brand and Meta ad account. Palmm can still install the OS for clients as a side door. Don’t spin out a company until 3–5 studios stay without Slack babysitting.
